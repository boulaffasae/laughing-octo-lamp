Before booking a car in Morocco, ask how the agency takes its security deposit. Choose a professional who explains the amount, possible deductions and release conditions in writing.

## Why look for an agency that does not require a cheque?

A cheque is not just paperwork. It can lead to payment being taken and a dispute about the amount claimed. A spoken promise not to cash it does not replace written terms. This guide recommends seeking a rental without a cheque; it does not determine the legal status of every practice. For a dispute, consult your bank or a Moroccan legal professional.

## How card preauthorisation works

Preauthorisation reserves an available amount on a card and differs from a charge. Ask the agency how much it will request, and ask your bank how this affects your spending limit. Release timing depends on the bank and transaction processing, so do not assume a universal deadline.

A deposit that is charged and later refunded is a different arrangement. Obtain a receipt, written refund terms and an explanation of the evidence needed for any deductions.

## Credit card or debit card?

Not all cards are accepted. Some agencies require a credit card in the main driver’s name and may reject debit cards. Get explicit confirmation for your card type. Do not send your full card number or security code through a message to obtain this confirmation.

## Questions before paying

- [ ] Can I rent without leaving a cheque?
- [ ] Which card type and cardholder are required?
- [ ] Will you reserve funds or take a payment?
- [ ] How much will be held, and which deductions are possible?
- [ ] What proves that the deposit was released or refunded?

## Returning the vehicle

Have the car’s condition, fuel and mileage recorded. Ask for an itemised invoice and evidence that the agency requested the release of the deposit. Keep the correspondence. If the hold remains visible, contact the agency and then your bank with the transaction reference.
