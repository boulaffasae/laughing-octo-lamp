Before paying for a car rental in Morocco, check that you meet the agency’s requirements for your chosen vehicle category. Ask for written confirmation before making a non-refundable booking.

## Is a licence held for 2 years compulsory?

Some agencies or categories require two years of driving experience. This is not a universal requirement: the agency, its insurer and the vehicle category determine the applicable conditions. Licence experience and minimum driver age are separate checks.

Ask when the licence must meet the threshold: at booking or at collection. If your licence has been renewed, check how to show its original issue date. For a foreign licence, confirm which documents are accepted and whether an international driving permit is needed.

## Your collection checklist

- [ ] Valid original driving licence with accepted driving experience.
- [ ] Valid identity card or passport.
- [ ] Minimum age confirmed for the booked category.
- [ ] Additional drivers named in the agreement.
- [ ] Accepted payment and deposit method confirmed.
- [ ] Written total price, excess and cancellation conditions.

## Ask a precise question

“I first received my licence on 10 September 2024. Can I collect this category on 1 November 2026, and which documents should I bring?” A precise question is more useful than a general assurance about rentals.

Check whether you are booking a model or a category. An Opel Corsa may be replaced with an equivalent vehicle. If you need an automatic, have the transmission confirmed in writing rather than relying on a photograph.

## Check the car before leaving

Record existing scratches, wheel damage and interior defects on the signed inspection report. With the agency’s agreement, keep photographs as well. Note the fuel level and odometer reading, and take a readable copy of the contract and assistance contact details.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a new driver rent a car?

Ask agencies about the conditions for your category before paying a non-refundable amount. Do not assume that another agency’s policy applies.

### Does having a licence for two years guarantee acceptance?

No. Age, documentation, payment and the deposit still need to satisfy the agency’s conditions.
