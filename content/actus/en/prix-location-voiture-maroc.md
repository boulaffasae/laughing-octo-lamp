How much should you budget for car rental in Morocco? The daily price is only the starting point. Dates, duration, city, insurance and extras can change the final bill. Compare written quotes for the same dates and vehicle category.

## Illustrative daily price table

These amounts are **budget examples for this guide**, not verified market rates or offers from Louma. Ask for a dated quote for your trip.

| Category             | Example model                   | Transmission | Example / day | Summer example (+50 DH) |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------ | ------------- | ----------------------- |
| Small car            | Opel Corsa or Peugeot 208       | Manual       | 250 DH        | 300 DH                  |
| Equivalent small car | Depending on the agency’s fleet | Automatic    | 350 DH        | 400 DH                  |

The Peugeot 205 is an older model, distinct from the Peugeot 208. If a listing offers a 205, check its year, maintenance and equipment. The models here do not imply availability.

## Allowing for summer prices

The extra 50 DH is a budgeting assumption, not a standard surcharge or a price cap. Demand, airport delivery and booking timing can produce a different increase. Compare insurance, mileage and return conditions as well as the headline rate.

## A 7-day budget example

At 250 DH a day, a manual costs **1,750 DH** for seven days. At 300 DH, it costs **2,100 DH**, an increase of 350 DH. An automatic at 350 DH totals **2,450 DH**; at 400 DH, **2,800 DH**. These examples exclude fuel and any charges not included in the quote.

## Fees to check in writing

- [ ] Taxes and insurance included in the displayed rate.
- [ ] Mileage allowance and excess kilometre charges.
- [ ] Delivery, collection location and out-of-hours fees.
- [ ] Additional driver, child seat and other options.
- [ ] Fuel policy, cleaning and late-return charges.
- [ ] Deposit, insurance excess and refund conditions.

## Manual or automatic?

An automatic can be convenient in city traffic. A manual may suit a tighter budget if you are comfortable driving one. Have the transmission written into your booking: a brand name alone does not identify the gearbox.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the deposit part of the rental price?

Separate the cost of the rental from money held as security. Ask whether the deposit is blocked or charged and how it is released.
