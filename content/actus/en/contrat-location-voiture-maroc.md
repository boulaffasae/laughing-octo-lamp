Your rental agreement records the responsibilities of the driver and the agency. Read it before signing, even for a short rental. Make sure the agreed quote and options appear clearly in the document.

## Identify the parties and vehicle

Check the agency’s name and contact information, your details, authorised drivers, registration number and vehicle category. Ask for missing or incorrect details to be corrected. Additional drivers must be declared according to the rental terms.

## Price, dates and return times

Separate the rental price, optional services and security deposit. Check the collection and return locations as well as the exact times.

**Illustrative clause:** “Rental from 1 November at 10:00 to 4 November at 10:00.” Ask what happens if you return at noon: a grace period, an hourly fee or another full day? Get the answer in writing.

## Mileage and fuel

**Illustrative clause:** “200 km per day, 2 DH per additional kilometre.” Over three days, the allowance would be 600 km. A 650 km trip would add 100 DH under this fictional clause. Check whether the allowance applies separately each day or across the whole rental.

With a full-to-full fuel policy, the return level must match the starting level. Ask how missing fuel and any refuelling service are charged.

## Insurance, excess and exclusions

Included insurance does not mean every type of damage is covered. Ask about the excess and exclusions. Tyres, glass, keys, unpaved-road use and undeclared drivers may have specific conditions. Any excess-reduction option should describe exactly what it changes.

The deposit is not automatically a limit on your liability. Check these amounts separately and ask which steps to follow after an accident or breakdown.

## Inspection checklist

- [ ] Existing scratches, dents and defects recorded at collection.
- [ ] Dated photographs kept and inspection report signed.
- [ ] Fuel level and odometer reading noted.
- [ ] Deposit amount and release method written down.
- [ ] Assistance number and accident procedure supplied.
- [ ] Complete contract copy provided to both parties.

## Before you sign

Do not sign an incomplete document or a clause you do not understand. Ask for an explanation in a language you can read, and keep any attached terms. The examples here are fictional and do not replace legal advice on your agreement.
